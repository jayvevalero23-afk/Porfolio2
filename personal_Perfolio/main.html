LILY-GUARD Flood Monitoring & Risk Assessment System

LILY-GUARD is a desktop-based flood monitoring and risk assessment application designed to help users monitor weather conditions and assess potential flood risks in selected locations. It features a user authentication system, location selection, weather monitoring, and simulated weather conditions for offline testing. Developed as a local software prototype, the system aims to provide an accessible way to explore weather-based monitoring and support community awareness of potential flooding.

Waveify

Waveify is a Spotify-inspired music player interface developed through AI-assisted vibe coding. I created it by describing my desired design and functionality through prompts, then refining the results until they matched my vision. It features a sleek, dark-themed layout with a sidebar for Home, Search, and Your Library. The home screen includes a personalized greeting, quick-play playlists such as Liked Songs and Late Night Chill, and a "Made for You" section featuring gradient album cards like Neon Skies and Midnight Drive. It also includes a bottom music player with playback controls, shuffle, repeat, a progress bar, and volume adjustment. Through this project, I explored how AI-assisted development can turn creative ideas into functional user interfaces while improving my design and development skills.
